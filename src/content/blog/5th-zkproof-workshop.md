The 2022 edition of [ZKProof Workshop](https://zkproof.org/workshop4/) just ended, and I loved every bit of it!

## The Great Sync

We had 100+ participants over 3 days, arriving to Tel-Aviv all the way from Korea, Japan, East and West-Coast USA, Canada, Australia and Europe. We got an overview of ZKP today, discussions on Plonk standardization, how to approach security, the state of programming languages and many other great presentations.

Special thanks to the ZKProof Editors (Eran Tromer, Luis Brandao, Mary Maller and Daniel Benarroch) for putting together such an [amazing agenda](https://zkproof.org/workshop4/agenda/)!

Despite the stress of it, it was a joy to organize! We have a very inclusive, fun and interesting community.

## Broad overview, times 3

Even though each of our keynote was a broad review of the space, we still had 3 distinct talks with almost no overlap! 🙂

- **Justin Thaler**, author of the most comprehensive book today on Zero-Knowledge, discussed the building blocks of current ZKP protocols before warning about a security concern that he saw across several academic papers.

- **Kobi Gurkan**, one of the most prolific technology leaders in the space, gave concrete examples of programming tools available for the beginning, and advanced, ZKP engineer.

- **Mary Maller** who leads the cryptography efforts at the Ethereum Foundation, provided an entry point for the ZKP-curious, with an up-to-date review of academic papers and recent advancement in the space.
